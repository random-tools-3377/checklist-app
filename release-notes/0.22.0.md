A planner for the coming days, and moving a goal to another day.

**New**

- **Plan tab.** See today and the next two weeks, a day at a time: what's due, at what time, goals for the week, and project deadlines. Like any tab, you can hide it in **Settings → Appearance → Tabs**.
- **Move to another day.** Tap **Move** on a goal in the planner, or next to **Skip** on Today, and pick another day of the same week (Monday to Sunday), from today on. It's free and needs no reason. If it isn't done on the new day, that's a miss as usual. A moved goal can be moved again, or back to its own day.
- Goals due a number of times a week or month don't need moving, so they don't show **Move**.
- The **Review** tab has a star icon now, and the calendar icon goes to **Plan**.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.22.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
