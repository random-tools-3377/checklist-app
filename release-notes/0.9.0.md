Routines: one goal with a checklist inside.

**New**

- **Routines.** In a habit's editor, under **Checklist**, tap **Add a checklist** and write its items, like water, stretch, make bed. On Today the routine shows its items with boxes to tick, one by one. When the last one is ticked, the routine is done and pays its points, with its celebration. Items pay nothing on their own.
- The routine's **Done** button ticks every item that's left in one go. Tap it again to take the whole routine back, or untick a single item.
- The items start fresh each time the routine is due. Skips, misses, streaks and reminders work as for any habit.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.9.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
