Badges for your milestones, and a weekly points target.

**New**

- **Badges.** Earn a badge for milestones like your first tick, a perfect day, a perfect week, a 7-day streak, 100 points earned or your first reward bought. There are 16 to earn. A new one shows as a card on Today. See them all from the top of the **Review** tab: the ones you have, with the day you earned each, and how to earn the rest. Once earned, a badge is yours to keep, even if you undo a tick.
- **Weekly points target.** Set a number of points to earn each week in **Settings → Weekly points target**. Today shows how far along you are, Monday to Sunday, and the weekly summary on Sunday says whether you reached it. Spending points in the shop doesn't count against it.
- Badges you've already earned are counted from your history, so you may find a few waiting the first time you open this version.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.13.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
