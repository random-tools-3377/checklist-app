Three extras to keep you going: a boost for the goal you miss most, a perfect-day bonus, and prices that rise when a reward is bought again.

**New**

- **Boost.** Each week, the goal you missed most over the last 4 weeks (at least twice) pays 50% more, all week. Today marks it with 🔥. It's chosen afresh every Monday.
- **Perfect-day bonus.** A day when everything due was done, none of it skipped, pays +10 when it ends. Today says when you're on track for one. It shows in the points history.
- **Prices rise on repeat buys.** A reward costs 25% more for each time it was already bought this week, and goes back to its price on Monday. The shop shows the higher price and why. Returning one takes it off the count and refunds what you paid.
- Turn each of these off, or change the perfect-day bonus, in **Settings → Difficulty**.

**Good to know**

- Backup files now include perfect-day bonuses. Older versions of the app can't read a backup made by this one.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.28.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
