Tablets, and phones turned on their side.

**New**

- **Tabs down the side.** On a tablet, or a phone turned on its side, the tabs sit in a strip down the left instead of along the bottom, leaving more room for your list.
- **Easier to read on big screens.** Lists, settings and editors keep a comfortable width in the middle instead of stretching right across a tablet. Today's background still fills the screen.
- **The focus timer fits sideways.** With the phone on its side, the clock sits on the left and the buttons beside it, so **Stop** and **Done now** are always on screen.
- **Celebrations fit too.** The big full-screen celebrations shrink a little on a phone on its side, so the words aren't cut off.

Anything you were typing stays put when you turn the phone.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.40.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
