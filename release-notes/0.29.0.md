Undo, and a quick settings tile.

**New**

- **Undo.** After you tick something, buy a reward or remove a goal, a bar at the bottom says what happened, with **Undo** for 5 seconds.
- **Quick settings tile.** Pull down the phone's quick settings, tap the pencil to edit them, and drag in **Tick next**. It shows the next goal due today, in Today's order, and a tap ticks it. Private goals behind the lock are left out.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.29.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
