Quick to-dos: one-off tasks you can add in a second.

**New**

- **To-dos on Today.** Type a to-do in the box under **To-dos** and tap **+**. It stays on Today until you tap **Done**, and pays its points (2 to start with). Tap **Done** again to take it back.
- **A date, if you want one.** Tap the calendar button next to the box, or tap a to-do, to give it a date, change its points or its icon, or make it private. A to-do with a date shows on Today and in the planner on that day. If it isn't done by the end of that day, it's a miss, with a reason, like any goal.
- **Without a date it's never missed.** It waits until you do it.
- **One allowance for all to-dos.** Between them, to-dos can be skipped or missed once a week before it costs points.
- To-dos are listed under **Goals** too, and **+** there can add one. Like Today's other sections, the **To-dos** section can be moved or hidden in **Settings → Appearance**.
- Backup files now include to-dos. Older versions of the app can't read a backup made by this one.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.23.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
