More for to-dos, and a list of what's done.

**New**

- **Steps on a to-do.** Give a to-do a few steps in its editor, like "Clear it" and "Carry it" for moving a desk. Tick them off on Today over as many days as it takes; ticking the last one does the to-do and pays its points. **Done** ticks all the steps at once.
- **A reminder on a dated to-do.** In a to-do's editor, once it has a date, set **Remind me at** for a notification on that day.
- **To-dos in the widget.** To-dos without a date show at the end of the calendar widget's list, with a tick box.
- **To-dos in the morning plan.** It says how many to-dos are waiting.
- **Done list.** **Goals → Done list** shows finished projects and done to-dos, newest first. Tap one to open it.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.25.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
