Skips that are fair both ways, misses you can put right, and a check before the day ends.

**Skips**

- **A skip never counts as done, and it isn't held against you either.** A skipped goal is simply left out of that day.
  - The main streak looks only at the rest of the day. A day where everything was skipped neither adds to it nor breaks it, and can't start a "you stopped for a while" check-in.
  - A day with a skip is never a perfect day, so it earns no perfect-day bonus or combo, and it ends the **Skip-free** streak.
  - In the Review, **done** means really ticked. A skip shows only under **Skipped**, so skipping can't make a goal look more consistent.
- **Take back a skip.** A skipped goal's card on Today shows **Take back the skip** until the day ends, and right after skipping you can tap **Undo**. Everything goes back as if you never skipped: the free skip, any points it cost, and its penalty task.
- **Ticking a skipped goal** the same day takes the skip back by itself.
- If a skip went over the limit, the penalty screen offers **Take back the skip** too.

**Misses**

- **The "you stopped for a while" check-in lists each goal missed**, by day, with its own **I forgot to tick it**. If that goes for all of them, the check-in goes away, with its penalty task.
- **Put a miss right later.** Open a miss from the Review (the Day view's **Missed** list, or a goal's row) or from its penalty's line in the points history. You can change its reason or note any time, and for 14 days you can still say **I did it, I forgot to tick it**.
- **A forgotten tick gives the day back in full.** If the day was perfect after all, its perfect-day bonus is paid, and your combo carries on from where it was.
- **Same reason for all:** with two or more misses waiting, one tap fills every card. Each can still be changed.
- A slip on a goal to quit now reads as a slip, with **Undo, I didn't slip**.

**Evening check**

- **One notification in the evening with what's still open today**, so you can do it before it counts as a miss. Set a time under **Evening check** in **Settings → Notifications**. It's off until you do.
- Nothing comes on a day when everything is done. Locked private goals are only counted, never named.
- On Android it has **Open Today** and a button to tick the first goal, and it updates as you get things done. On Windows it shows while the app is open.

**Best streaks**

- **Review → Stats** now shows your best-ever main streak beside the current one, and the best run of each other streak.
- Looking at an earlier week, month or year shows the longest each streak ran in that stretch.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.64.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.64.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.64.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: download `checklist-0.64.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
