Notes for your moods, what played a part, and a mood journal.

**Changed: the check-in once everything is done** (with **Mood check-in** on in Settings → Features)

- It now only says how your mood moved: "Up from 🙁 to 🙂.", "Same as this morning." or "Down from 🙂 to 🙁 — that's all right." It no longer says that getting things done is what changed it, because a lot more goes into a mood.
- Then it asks **What played a part?**

**New: what's behind a mood, and a note**

- **After you pick a face** in the morning, Today asks **What's behind it?** Once everything is done, the second check-in asks **What played a part?**
- Tap any that fit: **Slept well**, **Slept badly**, **Exercise**, **Outdoors**, **Friends and family**, **Alone time**, **Work**, **Health**, **Weather**, **Food**, **Money**, **Screen time**, **News**. Tap **+ Add your own** for something else.
- They're for the whole day, so you can add or take them off at either check-in.
- Each check-in has its own short note. Type it and tap **Save**. In the morning, tap **Done** when you've finished.

**New: Mood journal**

Every day you checked in, newest first: how you felt in the morning and later, what played a part, and your notes. Open it from the card once everything is done, from **Mood** in the Review, or from **Mood and your goals**.

- Tap a day to change its faces, what played a part, or its notes.
- Tap **Edit the list** to take things off the list, add your own, or put back the usual ones. Days you already picked something on keep it.

**More in Mood and your goals**

- **Things that go with a better mood** and **…a lower mood**: what you pick at the check-ins, compared the same way as your goals. For example: "Slept well: your mood is about 1.1 higher on days you note it."
- Everything there is worded as what goes together, like "Goals that go with a better mood". It doesn't say what causes what.

The journal is locked like your moods: behind the privacy lock if you lock reasons and notes, or the review. Notes never show in widgets or reminders. Your backup file now holds the notes and what played a part.

**New: put things in your own order**

- **Every card on Today moves on its own.** In **Settings → Appearance → Today's sections**, the mood check-in, your character, the daily quests, the race with last week, bets and the boss are now each their own part, so you can move any of them up or down with the arrows, or untick one to hide it. Until you do, Today looks the same as before.
- **The tabs along the bottom can be put in order too:** the arrows beside each tab in **Settings → Appearance** move it, and the one the app opens on is still yours to pick.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.61.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.61.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.61.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: download `checklist-0.61.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
