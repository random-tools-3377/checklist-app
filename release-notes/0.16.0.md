Today as a grid of big tiles, bigger or smaller cards and text, and a background behind Today.

**New**, in **Settings → Appearance → Today**

- **Grid.** Set **Layout** to **Grid** for big tiles, two to a row. Tap a tile to tick it, or to add one to a goal done several times. Hold a tile to open its full card, with Skip, Focus, notes, and a routine's checklist. Done tiles fill with colour.
- **Card size**: Compact, Comfortable (as before) or Large. In the grid it sets how tall the tiles are.
- **Text size**: Small, Normal, Large or Larger, on top of the phone's own text size.
- **Background**: a gradient of the app's colour, or **My photo**, picked with the Photo Picker. The photo is faded toward the background colour so text stays readable; choose how much. It's copied into the app and goes in your backup file.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.16.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
