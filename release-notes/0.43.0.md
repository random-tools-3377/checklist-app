Two more things the optional AI can do.

**New** (with **AI helper** on, and Checklist AI set up as in 0.42.0)

- **Suggest steps.** In a new or existing project, tap **Suggest steps** under the steps. The AI suggests steps from the project's name, like "plan a birthday party". Untick any you don't want and tap **Add these**: they go into the steps above, to change or remove like any other.
- **A note on your week.** In **Review → Week**, tap **Write a note**. The AI writes a few sentences about the week, from that week's numbers only: one thing that went well and one small change to try. Private goals aren't named, and the reasons for misses are left out while they're locked.

Everything is worked out on your phone. Without Checklist AI, these show where to get it, and nothing else changes.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.43.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.43.0.apk` too: the two always go together.
