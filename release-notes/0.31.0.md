A timeline of your day.

**New**

- **Timeline.** Tap **Timeline** next to the date on Today, or on a day in the **Plan** tab. The day is laid out by the hour: goals with a time sit at it, and the rest wait at the top under **Any time**.
- **Move a goal to another time, just for that day.** Hold a goal and drag it up or down; it snaps to the quarter hour. Or tap it and pick a time. Tap an **Any time** goal to give it a time that day. Reminders, pacing and the order on Today all follow the new time. **Usual time** puts it back.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.31.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
