Bet on yourself, and streak shields.

**New** (each an extra: turn it on in **Settings → Features**, under **Game**)

- **Bet on yourself.** On Today, tap **Place a bet**: pick a habit, how many times you'll do it this week, and a stake of 10, 25 or 50 points.
  - The stake comes off straight away.
  - Do it that many times this week and you get twice the stake back the moment you get there.
  - If the week ends short, the stake is gone.
  - One bet per habit a week, three at a time. Undoing the tick that won a bet takes the win back too.
- **Streak shields.** Every 30 days of main streak earns a 🛡️ shield, and you can hold 2.
  - A day that would break your main streak uses one up instead, and the streak carries on.
  - The streak card on Today shows your shields, the last day one saved your streak, and when the next one comes.
  - Shields count from the day you turn them on. Badges still count your streak without them.
