A simpler start, guides only when you want them, and bets taken out.

**Bets are gone**

- **There are no more bets.** The Bets switch, the Bets card on Today and the bet numbers in Points and rules are gone.
- **Any bet still open gives its points back** the first time the app opens. It shows in the points history as "Bet stake back".
- Past bets stay in your points history as they were. **Streak shields are still there**, now under their own heading in Points and rules.

**Guides when you want them**

- **Guides no longer pop up by themselves.** Tap **ⓘ** on any screen to read its guide. To have a tip show the first time each screen opens, turn on **Show a tip the first time each screen opens** in **Settings → Help and tips**.
- **Help and tips** now lists every guide in groups, like Basics, Goals, Money and Mood. It has a **search** box and an **Only extras that are on** switch.
- **New guides:** reminders, and every game extra (daily quests, beat last week, items and bosses, lucky ticks, combos, streak shields, the budget game, paths and titles).
- **Every guide ends with See also**, linking the parts that work together, and uses your own numbers from Points and rules.
- **More ⓘ buttons:** on the game cards on Today, on the misses, penalty and "you stopped for a while" screens, and on the focus timer.

**A simpler start**

- **New users get a short setup** instead of a tour, in three steps:
  - What would you like to use? To-dos, mood check-in, budget or the game.
  - A first goal: pick one of five ideas or add your own.
  - When your day starts and ends.

  Every step can be skipped. If you already have goals, you won't see it.
- **Empty screens say what to do next**, with a button, like "Nothing due today. Add a goal, then tick it off here."
- **How the app works** is still in Settings. If you already have goals, it asks before adding its examples, and **Undo** takes them away again.

**Completed and achieved**

- In the **Goals** tab, habits whose last day has passed and projects with every step done move into a **Completed** section at the bottom. Open it to look back. **Extend** carries a habit on, and **Remove** clears one away.
- Good things are worded as good things: projects are **Achieved**, challenges **Completed**, and the other two streaks are now called **Miss-free** and **Skip-free**.

**Finding things**

- **Settings search finds everything:** the extras on the Features page and every part of Appearance too ("budget", "quests", "dark mode", "confetti", "mood", "habit chains"…). It understands plurals and word forms, and each result opens right at that place. If nothing matches, it offers **Help and tips**.
- **Things for extras that are off stay out of the way:** Today's sections in Appearance, the weekly treat budget, and their numbers in Points and rules show once the extra is on.
- **Privacy** says plainly that the lock covers moods: the switch is now **Reasons and notes for skips, misses and moods**.

**Small tidying**

- **Text size** has its own heading in Appearance, since it changes the whole app.
- **My day starts at** opens at 7 am.
- **Goal colour dots** are bigger and have names for screen readers.
- **Every switch** in the goal and reward editors and the budget's work days toggles from its whole row.
- **On Windows**, the app says "computer" instead of "phone".

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.63.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.63.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.63.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: download `checklist-0.63.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
