A notification while you time something, and amounts more than once.

**New**

- **Timing notification.** While a goal is being timed with **▶ Start**, a quiet notification shows the time running. Tap **Stop** on it to say how much you did, without hunting for the goal in the app.
- **Amounts more than once.** A goal with an amount can now be done several times, like "1 mile, 3 times a week" or "10 pages, twice a day". Each whole amount counts as one time and pays its points; what you enter adds up across entries, so a 1.5-mile walk counts one time and half of the next. A bonus can be paid for doing all of them. Set it with **Times** in the editor, under the amount.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.21.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
