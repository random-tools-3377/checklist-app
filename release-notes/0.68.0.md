The rest of the money features, safer backups, a better Windows app, and "at most" goals.

**Money** (with **Budget** on)

- **Quick "+ Spent".** Note spending without opening the Budget tab: on Today's pay card, on the budget widget, or by holding the app icon and tapping **Spent**.
- **Bill reminders.** Each bill can remind you on the day, the day before, or 3 or 7 days before, like "Rent is due tomorrow, 650". With the budget locked it only says "A bill is due".
- **Regular money in.** Turn on **Money in** in the bill editor for income that comes regularly, like a pension. It's counted on its day by itself.
- **Pay changes from a date.** When you change your pay, tax or hours, the app asks from when: Today, The 1st of this month, or From the start. Earlier days keep the pay they had, so past months don't change after a pay rise.
- **Spending from savings.** The spending form has **From savings**: the money comes out of a saving goal and doesn't count as this month's spending.

**Shop**

- **Star a reward to save for.** Tap the star on a reward. Today's balance card then shows a bar and "about 9 more days at your usual pace", or "You can buy it now". The morning plan mentions it too.
- **One "days of earning" figure.** The shop, the balance check, the reward editor and Today now all show the same number of days for the same price.

**Backups**

- **Importing is safer.** The confirmation shows what's in the file and what it replaces, with an **Export first** button. On Android with a backup folder set, a "Before import" copy is saved there first.
- **Passphrase.** **Protect backup files with a passphrase** in Settings → Backup encrypts your exports and automatic backups. A forgotten passphrase can't be recovered, so keep it somewhere safe.
- **Erase everything and start fresh**, at the bottom of Settings → Backup. It asks twice and offers to export first. Backup files you've saved stay.

**Windows**

- **Reminders keep coming with the window closed.** Closing the window keeps Checklist running by the clock, with **Open Checklist** and **Quit** on its icon. You can turn this off, or turn on **Start when I sign in to Windows**, in Settings → Notifications.
- **Automatic backup folder**, as on Android: pick a folder and a dated file is saved there each day you use the app. The last 7 are kept.

**"At most" goals**

- **A goal to stay under**, like no more than 2 coffees. Turn on **At most goals** in Settings → Features, then turn on **At most (stay under a limit)** in a new habit's editor.
- Tap **+1** on Today each time. The card shows what's used and what's left.
- A day at or under the limit counts as done and pays when the day ends, or straight away with **That's all for today**.
- Going over is a miss straight away. **Take back the last one** undoes a slip of the finger.

Your backup file changed to hold the new details. Older backup files still import.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.68.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.68.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.68.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: if Checklist is running, quit it first. Then download `checklist-0.68.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
