Categories for your goals, and charts in the review.

**New**

- **Categories.** Give a goal a category, like "Health", "Home" or "Work", when you add or edit it. Type a new one or tap one you already use.
- **One category at a time on Today.** When your goals have categories, chips at the top of Today show just one of them. Tap **All** to see everything again.
- **Charts in the review.** The week and month views show each day as a bar: how much of what was due got done, and the points you earned. Points lost for going over the limit show in red below the line.
- **By category in the review.** How much got done in each category, and the points it earned.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.12.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
