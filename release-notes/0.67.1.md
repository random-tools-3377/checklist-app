The Money game explains its daily figure, and you can set your own.

**Money game** (on the Budget tab)

- **The card no longer reads like a spending target.** It now says "Spent today: $0.00" and, under it, "Stay under $85.12 for a good money day", with where that figure comes from: this month's pay, less bills, spread over its days.
- **Set your own daily amount.** Tap **Set my own daily amount** on the card and type the figure you want to stay under each day. **Work it out from my pay** puts it back.
- How points are earned is unchanged: more for a day with no spending, some for a day under the line, none for a day over it.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.67.1.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.67.1.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.67.1.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: download `checklist-0.67.1.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
