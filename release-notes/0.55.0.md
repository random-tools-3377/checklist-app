The app comes to the iPhone.

**New: Checklist for iPhone** (`checklist-0.55.0.ipa`, below)

The same app, with the same screens, rules and points, now on the iPhone too. Each phone keeps its own data; to move yours across, export it on one and import it on the other.

**Installing it on an iPhone**

Apple only lets you install an app that isn't on the App Store if it's signed with your Apple ID. SideStore or AltStore does that for you, for free, but the app has to be refreshed once a week.

1. **Set up SideStore once, with a computer.** Follow the steps at sidestore.io on a Windows PC or a Mac. It puts SideStore on your iPhone using your own Apple ID. After that, you don't need the computer. (AltStore, from altstore.io, works too, but it needs the computer each week to refresh.)
2. **Turn on Developer Mode** if the iPhone asks: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode, then restart.
3. **Download the app.** Open this Releases page in Safari on the iPhone and tap `checklist-0.55.0.ipa`. It goes into Files → Downloads.
4. **Install it.** Open SideStore, go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick the file.
5. **Refresh every week.** Open SideStore and tap **Refresh All** before the 7 days are up. If you forget, the app won't open until you refresh it. Your data stays safe either way.

**Updating:** download the new `.ipa` from a later Release and install it the same way. Your data stays.

**Moving your data from Android:** on Android, Settings → Export everything, then send the file to yourself however you like. On the iPhone, open Settings → Import a backup and pick it.

**Not on the iPhone yet:** widgets, the AI helper and voice, your phone's calendar, the automatic backup folder, changing the app icon, Done and Skip buttons on reminders, and the morning plan and weekly summary notifications. Reminders at goal times work.

**On Android**, nothing changes in this version: the app was rebuilt so the iPhone can share its screens.

**Installing on Android**

Download `checklist-0.55.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.55.0.apk` too.
