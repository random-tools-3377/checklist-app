Gap check-ins, the review screen and stats.

**New**

- **Gap check-ins.** When it looks like you stopped for a while (2 or more days in a row with nothing ticked, or 50 or more points lost to the limit within 7 days), the app asks what happened the next time it opens, before anything else. One reason and an optional note cover the whole stretch, and there's one penalty task for it instead of one for each miss. Points still come off for each miss in it. Change both numbers in **Settings → Gaps**.
- Gaps are marked in the **points history**, with what you wrote.
- **Review**, a new tab. Look at a day, a week or a month, and step back to earlier ones:
  - points earned, spent and lost
  - each goal: done, skipped and missed, and its longest run
  - which reasons came up most
  - gaps, with what you wrote
  - a month calendar, with gaps marked
  - stats: most consistent, most ticked, most skipped and most missed goals, the best and hardest days of the week, points per goal, and your streaks
- **More for the privacy lock.** In **Settings → Privacy** you can now also lock the reasons and notes for skips and misses, the gap check-ins, and the review and stats.

Gaps are looked for from the day you first open this version, never further back.
