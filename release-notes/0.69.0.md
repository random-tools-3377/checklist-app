Nudges when a goal is late, to help you get it done.

**Late nudges**

- **When a goal is late, the app keeps at it.** A habit with a set time, or its own Finish by time, gets a nudge once it's 30 minutes late, then about every 30 minutes until it's done, skipped or your day ends (12 at most).
- **Every nudge reads differently.** They take turns between four kinds:
  - **Encouraging:** "Still time for Reading. You've got this."
  - **What you'd gain:** the points it pays, your streak, or how close your starred reward is. Only real numbers.
  - **Make it smaller:** "Just the first 5 minutes counts as a start."
  - **Playful:** something light.
- The title says how late it is, like "Reading · 40 min late".
- **Stop for today.** On Android each nudge has **Done** (or **+1**), **Stop for today**, and on a "make it smaller" nudge for a goal with a length, **Focus**, which opens its focus timer. In the app, a late goal's card on Today has **No more nudges today**.
- **A switch for each goal:** **Nudge me when it's late** in the goal's editor, under Reminders.
- **Switches for all of them:** **Nudges when something is late** in **Settings → Notifications**, with a switch for each kind of message under it.
- **Your numbers:** how late before the first nudge, how often, and the most per day are in **Settings → Points and rules**, under Late nudges.
- On Android, nudges are their own notification type, **Late nudges**, so you can make them quieter in the phone's settings without touching your other reminders.
- A private goal behind the lock is never named in a nudge.
- Once a goal is late, nudges take the place of "keep reminding" for it, so you don't get both.
- Not for projects, to-dos, goals to quit, "at most" goals, or goals counted per week, month or year.
- On iPhone, nudges come without buttons. On Windows they show while Checklist is running.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.69.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.69.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.69.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: if Checklist is running, quit it first. Then download `checklist-0.69.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
