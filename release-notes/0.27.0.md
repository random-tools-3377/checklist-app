A balance check for your prices and points, and savings interest.

**New**

- **Balance check.** In the **Shop**, tap **Balance check** to see:
  - what you usually earn a day and a week
  - how many days each reward takes to earn, with ones that may be too cheap (under half a day) or out of reach (over two months), and a suggested price to use with one tap
  - goals done nearly every time over the last 4 weeks, which could pay less or be made harder, and goals missed half the time or more, where more points might help, each with a suggestion to use
- **Savings interest.** At the end of each week, unspent points grow by 2%, rounded down, to help save for big rewards. It's paid on Mondays and shows in the points history. Change it or turn it off in **Settings → Difficulty → Savings interest** (off, 1%, 2% or 5%).

**Good to know**

- Backup files now include savings interest. Older versions of the app can't read a backup made by this one.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.27.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
