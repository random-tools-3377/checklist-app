Reminders are now about the goal and why it's good for you, not the points.

**Why it's good**

- **Each goal can say why it's good, in your own words.** A new box, **Why it's good**, sits under the name in the habit and project editors. Write something like "so I sleep better". It's optional, and only you write it: the app and the AI never fill it in.
- **A goal's reminders start with it.** A reminder for Stretch could read "so I sleep better · Now · 10 min". Its title is still the goal's icon and name.
- **Reminders no longer say what a goal pays.** "Last one for today" and the streak line stay, because they're about getting it done. A goal without a reason reads as before, just without the points.
- **A project's deadline reminders** start with its reason too.
- Points still show once something is ticked, like "ticked, +5", and everywhere in the app.

**Late nudges**

- **"What you'd gain" is now "Why it's good".** These nudges use the goal's own reason and your streak, like "Why Stretch: so I sleep better. It helps keep your 12-day streak going too." They never mention points or rewards.
- A goal with no reason and no streak gets an encouraging nudge instead.
- If you had turned this kind of nudge on or off, that stays as you set it. The switch is in **Settings → Notifications**, under **Nudges when something is late**.
- While the review is behind the privacy lock, nudges don't mention your streak, the same as other reminders.

**Privacy**

- A private goal behind the lock is still never named in a reminder or nudge, and its reason isn't shown either.
- No widget shows a goal's reason.

**Also**

- On iPhone and Windows, a goal's reminder now gives the same details as on Android: how long it takes and how many are done.
- Backups now include each goal's reason. Backups from earlier versions still import; their goals simply have no reason yet. A backup from this version can't be imported into an older version of the app.
- The morning plan, the evening check and the Sunday summary haven't changed.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.72.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.72.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.72.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: if Checklist is running, quit it first. Then download `checklist-0.72.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
