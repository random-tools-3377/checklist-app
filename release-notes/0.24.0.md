A short guide for getting started, search in Goals, and shortcuts on the app's icon.

**New**

- **A guide.** Someone opening the app for the first time sees a welcome card on Today. **Show me around** goes through what the app does in six short pages. At the end you can add a few example goals and rewards to try, or start from scratch. The guide is also in **Settings → How the app works**.
- **Example goals.** A habit done 8 times a day, one with pages to read, one 3 times a week, a morning routine, a project, a to-do and two rewards. They're ordinary goals: change or remove them any time.
- **Search in Goals.** With more than one goal, a search box sits at the top of **Goals**. Type a word from a goal's name or category.
- **Shortcuts.** Hold the app's icon on the home screen for **New to-do**, **New goal**, **Plan** and **Shop**. You can drag one onto the home screen too.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.24.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
