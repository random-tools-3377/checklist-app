Your character now talks to you.

**New** (turn on **Character check-ins** in Settings → Features, with **Character and skills**)

- **Its face follows your day**: calm, happy, proud when everything's done, tired when it's late with a lot left, and worried after a slip.
- **It checks in when things slip.** After a day that got away, or a few days away, it doesn't scold: it welcomes you back and suggests one small thing to start with, the quickest goal left. Tap the button under it to tick that goal straight from the character's card.
- **It asks how you feel** (with **Mood check-in** on), right on its card, and answers:
  - on a hard day it's gentle, says rest counts too, and offers just one small thing if you'd like;
  - on a good day it's glad and cheers you on.
- **It jumps for joy** at a new level, and when everything due today is done.

Misses before you started using the app never count as slips.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.51.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.51.0.apk` too.
