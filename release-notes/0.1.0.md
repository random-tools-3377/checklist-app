The first version to try: goals, ticking, points and the shop.

**What you can do**

- Add **habits** that repeat every day, week or month. A habit can be done several times per period, like 8 glasses of water: each tick pays, and finishing all of them pays a bonus.
- Add **projects** made of steps. Each step pays, and finishing the project pays a bonus. A project can have a deadline (it is shown, but nothing happens yet when it passes).
- **Tick** goals on the Today screen to earn points. To undo, tap a ticked goal again, or use the − button on a counted one. Undoing takes the points back.
- Stock the **shop** with your own rewards and prices, and buy them with your points. A bought reward waits under **Bought** until you mark it used.
- A reward you can't afford yet shows how far you have got. Once you have some history, each price also shows roughly how many days of your usual earnings it costs.
- Small **celebrations**: a burst and a buzz when you tick, the full screen when you finish a project, and one of its own when you buy a reward.
- In **Settings** (the gear on the Today screen), choose when your day ends, for example 4 am, so a late night still counts for that day.

**Not in this version yet**

- Skips, misses, the allowance and penalties.
- Gap check-ins and the review screen.
- The home-screen widget and reminders.
- Celebration GIFs, and exporting or importing your data.

**Private and offline**

The app has no internet access and is left out of phone backups. Everything stays on the phone. Until export is added, uninstalling the app deletes your goals and points.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.1.0.apk` below on the phone, open it, and allow installing from your browser if Android asks. Later versions install over this one and keep your data.
