Celebration GIFs.

**New**

- **A GIF for each goal.** In a habit's or project's editor, under "Celebration when it's finished", pick one of the GIFs that come with the app (confetti, a star, fireworks, or a big tick), or **Your own GIF…** to choose one from your phone. When you finish that goal, its GIF fills the screen. Tap to close it. Goals left on "The usual" keep the quick burst.
- Choosing your own GIF uses Android's Photo Picker: the app gets that one file and no access to your other photos. It keeps a copy, so the GIF still works if you delete the original.
- Your own GIFs go into the backup file, and come back when you import it.

GIFs move on Android 9 and later. On Android 8 they show as a still picture.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.6.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
