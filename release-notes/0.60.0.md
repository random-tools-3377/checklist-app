The app on Windows, a second mood check-in once everything is done, and which goals lift your mood.

**New: Checklist for Windows** (`checklist-0.60.0.msi`, below)

The same app in a window on a Windows computer, with the same screens, rules and points.

- **Installing:** download `checklist-0.60.0.msi` below and open it. It isn't signed, so Windows may say it protected your PC: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs for your account, with a Start menu entry and a desktop shortcut. A newer `.msi` installs over the old one and keeps your data.
- **Your data stays on that computer**, separate from your phone's. To move it across, use **Settings → Export everything** on one and **Import a backup** on the other.
- **Reminders show only while the app is open**, from the notification area by the clock. There are no widgets, no phone calendar, no AI and no automatic backup folder on Windows, and the privacy lock opens with a note, because the app can't ask for the computer's own lock.
- It never goes online, like the phone apps.

**New: how do you feel now?** (with **Mood check-in** on in Settings → Features)

- **Once everything due today is done**, Today asks again: **How do you feel now?**, with the same five faces. If the day started low (😞 or 🙁), it says so first, and asks gently.
- **It answers you**: if your mood went up, it says so ("Up from 🙁 to 🙂 — getting it all done helped"). If it's still heavy, that's all right: you did everything anyway, and that counts. Then one line for tomorrow, and the goals that usually lift you.
- It asks once a day. Tap **Not now** to leave it until tomorrow, or **Close** once you've read the answer.
- It only asks on days you answered the morning check-in. The review shows both moods, like 🙁→🙂.

**New: Mood and your goals**

Open it from the card above, or from **Mood** in the Review. After a couple of weeks of check-ins, it shows:

- **What lifts you**: goals you feel better on days you do, in plain words, like "On days you do it, your mood is about 0.8 higher".
- **What weighs on you**: goals that go with a lower mood. No blame: some goals are just hard. Each one has up to three ways to make it lighter:
  - **Do it right after** a goal that lifts you. With **Habit chains** on, one tap sets that up.
  - **Make it smaller**: **Edit it** opens the goal.
  - **Pay more for it**: one tap makes it pay half as much again.
  - **Get it out of the way early.**
- On a morning that starts low, Today suggests a goal to **start with** that usually lifts you.

It looks at the last 90 days and needs at least 3 days each way before it says anything; both numbers can be changed in **Settings → Points and rules**, under **Mood and your goals**. It shows what goes together, not what causes what. Like your moods, it sits behind the privacy lock if you lock reasons and notes, or the review. Private goals are left out while locked.

Your backup file now holds both moods each day.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.60.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.60.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.60.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: see above.
