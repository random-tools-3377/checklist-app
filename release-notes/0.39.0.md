A spreadsheet of your points history, and restoring an automatic backup.

**New**

- **Points history as a spreadsheet.** In **Settings → Backup**, **Export history as a spreadsheet** saves every change to your balance as a CSV file, with the note on each tick. It opens in Sheets, Excel or Numbers. You choose where it goes. It is for looking at, not for importing back: for that, use **Export everything**.
- **Restore an automatic backup.** With the automatic backup on, **Settings → Backup** has **Restore an automatic backup**. It lists the dated backups in your folder, newest first. Pick one and you see what's in it before anything changes, just like importing a file.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.39.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
