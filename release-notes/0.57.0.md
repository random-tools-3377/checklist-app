A budget, with your pay filling up as you work.

**New: Budget** (turn it on in Settings → Features → Budget; a Budget tab appears)

- **Your pay, filling up.** Tap **Set up pay** (or **Pay and hours** later). Put in what you're paid, per hour, day, week, month or year, and your usual week: which days you work, your start and stop times, and any unpaid break. If tax comes off, put in the percentage you keep. The **Today** card then fills up with money from your start time to your stop time, live, with how long is left. **This month's pay** shows what you've earned so far out of the month's pay.
- **A different day.** **Change today** (or **Change a day…**) lets you give one day other hours, or make it a day off, paid or unpaid. The rest of the week stays as usual.
- **Spending and money in.** The **Spent** button adds what you spent: how much, what for, a note and the day. **Add money in** is for anything else you get, like a gift. Tap any of them to change or remove it.
- **Categories.** Food, Home, Bills, Transport, Fun, Health, Shopping, Rewards and Other to start with. **Edit** renames them, changes their icons and order, adds new ones, and gives any of them a monthly limit with a bar that turns red when you go over.
- **Rewards.** A reward in the shop with a money value counts as spent, under Rewards, as soon as you buy it. Returning it takes it back off.
- **What's left.** The month adds up your pay so far, other money in, what you've spent, what's left now and, if nothing else is spent, at the end of the month.
- **Hours of work.** With your pay set, spending and shop rewards that cost money show how long they take to earn, like "≈ 2 h 15 min of work".
- **Earlier months:** the arrows at the top go back a month at a time.
- **Private if you like:** Settings → Privacy can put the budget behind the lock. It's also in the backup file.

Everything is typed in by you; the app still never goes online.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.57.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.57.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.57.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.
