An app that reacts to how your day is going, a character that's alive, and a helper you can ask.

**Ask a question**

- **A page where you type a question and get an answer.** Open **Settings → Help and tips → Ask a question**, or with the AI helper on, tap 💬 at the top of Today.
- Ask about **your day** ("What's left today?", "How is Reading going?"), about **the app** ("What does a skip cost?", "How do I back up?"), or for **guidance** ("How do I get better at Fitness?", and the same for streaks, pace, points and rewards, money, mood and focus).
- Under each answer are your own figures, **From:** links to the guide it came from, and buttons that take you there.
- It works without the AI, also on iPhone and Windows. With the AI ready, the AI puts the answer into words. The app looks up every fact itself, so the numbers are always your real ones.
- Typing "done reading" or "add buy milk" works there too, and still asks first.
- Anything behind your privacy lock stays out until you unlock. The conversation is forgotten when you leave the page.

**How am I doing?**

- **A summary whenever you want it:** the **How am I doing?** button under the balance on Today and in the Review's Week, and **How is it going?** on each goal's page. It shows today, this week beside last week, your streak, the goal going best, the one that could use a hand, and one next step with a button.
- **A note on your week** now always shows a short note with one suggestion and a button, such as **Make it smaller** or **Pay a little more**. The AI's note stays once written.

**The AI helper**

- **Describe it** reads days, times, "3 times a week", "for 30 minutes" and "20 pages" out of your sentence itself, then lists what it understood, what's still to set, and suggested points. It works without the AI too.
- **Suggest steps** works for to-dos as well, with a hint box and **Suggest different ones**.
- While the AI thinks, each card shows how long it's been, with **Stop waiting**. If it can't answer, it says what to try in plain words.
- Voice understands "what's next", "what's left" and "how am I doing", and offers today's goals as buttons when it didn't catch the name.

**Today reacts**

- **Each tick says what it changed:** "2 left today", "That was the last one", "Today now counts for your streak: day 13", "New best: 9 in a row". **Undo** says what went back.
- **What's within reach:** a line at the top of Today names the closest win, like "1 more for a perfect day (+10)" or "3 ticks from the 100 ticks badge".
- **The streak card** says "Today counts: day 13" or what today still needs, with your best beside it.
- **A morning card** says what yesterday came to and what was paid overnight.
- **The pace card** says when you're ahead. When time is short it names the shortest goal to start with and offers to move one to another day this week. It has a thin "3 of 7 done" bar.
- **On a low-mood morning** the pace card says "Whatever you manage today is fine." instead of a red warning. No rules change.
- **When everything is done,** a recap card shows what you earned, what tonight adds, a highlight, and a peek at tomorrow.
- **A day with nothing due** says "Nothing due today. Enjoy it." and shows tomorrow's first few.
- With **Combos** on, Today shows the combo and what a perfect day pays tonight. With **Beat last week** on, the race card says what would take the lead.

**The character**

- **It's alive:** it breathes and blinks, waves when you first open Today, nods at a tick, stretches in the morning and dances when everything is done. It stays still when your device has animations turned off.
- **Five new faces:** sleepy, surprised, determined, relieved and cheering.
- **It talks about what just happened:** "Reading done. That's 4 of 6." or "That one was running late and it's done. Phew."
- **It notices** skill levels, new gear, lucky ticks, finished quests, boss hits and a shield saving your streak.
- **It follows your day:** good morning when your day starts, sleepy and gentle near your day's end, and "Back on track!" the day after a rough one.
- **Its suggestion says why:** "Why this one: it's the quickest one left and you usually do it around now."
- Its lines change from day to day and use your real numbers.

**Badges**

- **Badges show how far along they are,** like "73 of 100 ticks", with the closest one first as **Next up**.
- **A new badge, Bounced back,** for reaching your day's target again right after a day that fell short. The Review's Stats count your comebacks.

**Money and amounts**

- **After adding spending,** the bar says where that leaves you, like "Food: £42 left of £200 this month", with **Undo**.
- **Fewer taps:** amount and Spent boxes open with the keyboard up, the keyboard's Done key saves, your usual amounts are one tap, and Spent starts on the category you used last.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.70.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.70.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.70.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: if Checklist is running, quit it first. Then download `checklist-0.70.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
