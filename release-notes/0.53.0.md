A new widget to get through your day from the home screen, and a fix for the widgets.

**New: the Checklist today widget**

Add it like any widget: hold an empty spot on the home screen, choose **Widgets**, then **Checklist today**.

- **Up next** sits at the top, big, with its own button. Tap **Done** and the next one moves up.
- **The rest of the day** follows in the same order as Today in the app, each with its time and button:
  - **Done** for most goals
  - **+1** for a goal done several times a day, like brushing your teeth
  - **✓ Water**, **✓ Stretch**… for a routine, one item at a time
  - **Avoided** for a goal to quit
- **A time with "~"** is roughly when to get to it. A time in red means it's late.
- **Done ones fold to the bottom**, crossed off. Tap one to take it back.
- **Once everything is done**, it shows what's on tomorrow.
- **Make it small** to see only what's up next and what comes after. Make it taller for the whole day.

**Fixed**

- **Ticking on the calendar widget** now shows on it straight away. Before, it could keep showing "0 of 2" until later, even though the tick was saved and the streak widget showed it.
- **Goals done more than once a day** get a **+1** button on the calendar widget, instead of a box that looked ticked after the first time.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.53.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.53.0.apk` too.
