Your character, drawn, with gear that comes from your skills.

**New** (with **Character and skills** on, in **Settings → Features**)

- **Your character, drawn.** It's at the top of the character page, and small beside your level on Today.
- **Style it your way.** Under **Style** on the character page, pick from 6 skin tones, 6 hair styles, 6 hair colours and 3 kinds of eyes.
- **Gear from your skills.** Each skill earns a piece at levels 5, 10 and 20, and your character wears it:
  - 🏃 Fitness: trainers, a headband, a gold medal
  - 💧 Health: a water bottle, a healthy glow, a heart
  - 📚 Mind: glasses, a book, a graduation cap
  - 🏠 Home: an apron, a spatula, a potted plant
  - 💼 Work: a name badge, a briefcase, a blazer
  - 🎨 Creative: a beret, a paintbrush, a scarf
- **Your leading skill shows.** Where two pieces go in the same place, like two hats, the stronger skill's is worn. Your shirt takes the colour of the skill you're best at. The character page shows what each skill earns next.
