Ideas to level up each skill.

**New** (with **Character and skills** on)

- **💡 Ideas for every skill.** On the character page, each skill has an **Ideas** button. At the top, **Level up next** points to the skill furthest behind.
- **Build on what you do.** Your own goals in that skill can be stepped up a little: a few minutes longer, a bit more of an amount, or once more a week. After you save a step-up, the next one is ready.
- **Try something new.** Activities for each skill, suited to its level: getting started, building up, then pushing further. **More ideas** always brings more, and **Not for me** hides one you don't want.
- **New ideas from the AI.** With the AI helper and Checklist AI ready, **Make new ideas** asks it for fresh ones each time, different from those already shown.
- **Adding an idea** opens it in the goal editor, filled in with its skill and suggested points. Change anything before you save. Nothing is added until you do.
- **On Today**, once everything is done or nothing is due, your character offers ideas for the skill furthest behind (with **Character check-ins** on).

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.52.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.52.0.apk` too.
