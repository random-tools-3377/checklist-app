Daily quests, combos, and a race against last week.

**New** (each an extra: turn it on in **Settings → Features**, under **Game**)

- **Daily quests.** Three small quests each day, picked from the goals you have due, like "Tick 2 goals before noon", "Do 📖 Read", "Do all 2 🏃 Fitness goals", "Finish everything due by 8 pm" or "Earn 30 points from goals". They show on Today with how far along each is. A finished quest pays straight away: 5 points, or 10 for finishing everything by 8 pm.
- **Combos.** Every perfect day in a row (everything due done, nothing skipped) builds a combo, paying 2 points for each day of it, up to 20 a day. It shows in your points history as "Combo ×3" and so on. A day left undone ends the combo; a day with nothing due doesn't count either way.
- **Beat last week.** Today shows how many points you've earned this week against last week by the same time, like "15 points ahead of last week".

Next: items from your ticks, lucky ticks, and a boss each week.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.47.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.47.0.apk` too.
