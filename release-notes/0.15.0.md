Your own colours: light, dark or pure black, any colour for the app, and a colour for each goal.

**New**

- **Appearance**, at the top of **Settings**.
- **Light, dark, phone or black.** Phone follows the phone's own dark mode, as before. Black is dark on a pure black background.
- **The app's colour.** Pick a ready-made one (Garden, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Lavender, Berry), match your wallpaper (Android 12 and later, as before), or tap **Any colour…** for the colour picker: a colour square, a hue slider and a hex code box, with your recent colours. The whole app takes its colours from the one you pick, and keeps text readable. A preview shows how it will look before you tap **Use it**.
- **A colour for each goal.** In a goal's editor, under **Colour**, pick one of the colours shown or any colour from the picker. It shows as a strip on the goal's card on Today and in the Goals list, and as a bar in the calendar widget.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.15.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
