A page for every goal, a review that shows more, a fuller mood journal, and focus time that's kept.

**A page for each goal**

- **Tap a goal in the Goals list to open its own page.** It shows a calendar of its done (✓), skipped (–) and missed (✕) days, its current and best run, how often it got done in the last 30 and 90 days, its ticks and points, and its reasons and notes. **Edit** at the top opens the editor.
- It also opens from **History** on a goal's card on Today, and from the goal's row in the Review.
- Reasons and notes on it stay behind the lock if you lock them, and a private goal's page asks to unlock.

**Review**

- **Tap a day** on the month calendar or a chart to open that day. Switching between Day, Week, Month and Year keeps the date you're looking at.
- **Finished** lists projects achieved, to-dos done, and what goals with an amount came to, like "Reading: 312 pages, 9 h 40 min".
- **Focus time** shows how long you spent with the focus timer, in total and for each goal.
- **Mood** is now a short summary: your average, how it compares with the stretch before, and what you noted most, with a small face on each day of the month. **See the notes** opens the journal on those days.

**Mood journal**

- **Add a day** you missed, or **delete** one logged by mistake.
- **Tap a chip** to see only the days you noted it, or **search** your notes.
- **Tap "Today: 😐" on Today** to change today's mood, what's behind it and your notes, and to reach the journal any day. **Check in now** takes back "Not today".
- **Mood reminder:** a daily nudge at a time you pick in **Settings → Notifications**. It only asks how you're feeling, and never shows a mood or a note.
- **A late first check-in asks once.** If you first check in after everything is done, Today doesn't ask a second time.
- **On a low morning** there's one "start with" suggestion instead of two, and it may name a reward you could look forward to.
- **Mood and your goals:** "Pay more" now has **Undo**, and **Highlight days I noted something** marks what comes only from days you noted something.

**Focus timer**

- **The time you really focus is kept.** Pauses don't count.
- **Optional breaks:** pick 5, 10 or 15 minutes under **Break after a focus session** in Settings, and the timer offers a break when a session ends.

**More spreadsheets**

- **The goal record:** each goal each day, done, skipped or missed, with the reason (**Settings → Backup**).
- **The mood journal**, from its page.
- **A month's budget**, from the budget report.

**New badges**

- For reaching a saving goal, money streaks of 7 and 30 days, a month within every spending limit, and 30 mood check-ins. They show while those extras are on.

Your backup file now also holds your focus time. Older backup files still import.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.66.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.66.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.66.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: download `checklist-0.66.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
