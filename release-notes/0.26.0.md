Suggested points and prices, and a difficulty setting that can get harder over time.

**New**

- **How hard is it?** In a goal's editor, pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Very hard. The app suggests the points: 2, 5, 10 or 20, plus a little more for a goal that takes longer, shared out over a goal done several times. Tap **Use it** to take the suggestion.
- **What is it worth?** In a reward's editor, pick a size (small treat, medium, big or huge), or type what it costs in money. The app suggests a price from what you usually earn: a small treat is about a day's worth, a huge one about six weeks'. For money, it asks once what you'd spend on treats in a normal week.
- **Difficulty.** In **Settings → Difficulty**, pick from Very easy to Very hard. Easier levels make goals pay more and allow more free skips and misses. Harder ones pay less and allow fewer. Normal is as you set each goal. Prices and penalties don't change.
- **Get harder over time.** Also in **Settings → Difficulty**: pick where to start, where to end up and over how many weeks, like Easy to Normal over 6 weeks. It steps up a level at a time on Mondays. Stop it any time, or just pick a level.
- Today shows what each goal pays at this week's difficulty.

**Good to know**

- Backup files now include each goal's difficulty and each reward's value. Older versions of the app can't read a backup made by this one.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.26.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
