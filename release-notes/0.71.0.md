Movement everywhere, the character on more screens, and reminders that follow your day.

**Animation and feel**

- **Ticked goals glide** to their new place instead of jumping. Rows glide in Goals, the shop and the points history too.
- **Bars fill and the balance counts up** to its new number.
- **The Done button pops,** and "2 of 8" rolls to "3 of 8".
- **Cards on Today fold open and closed,** and screens slide in and out. Today and Goals come back to where you had scrolled.
- **Different buzzes:** a light click for +1, a fuller buzz for finishing a goal, a soft double tap when something is refused, and a small tick when a tick is taken back. All follow the **Buzz** switch.
- **Swipe a card right to tick it.** It does exactly what the card's button does, with the same Undo. It never skips. The switch is **Swipe a card to tick it** in **Settings → Appearance**, on to start.
- **Drag to reorder.** Hold and drag goals and rewards in Reorder, Today's sections and tabs in Appearance, and budget categories. The arrows are still there.
- **No blank flash:** screens show faint card shapes for the moment before they load, and the Review keeps the old week dimmed until the new one is ready.
- **Less motion:** a switch in **Settings → Appearance** that turns all of this movement off. It starts on your device's own setting.

**The character on more screens**

- **Its own page:** it shows today's face, waves when the page opens, and answers a tap with a hop and a line about your next level or next gear.
- **Kind company** at the top of the misses, penalty and "you stopped for a while" screens, with one reassuring line. It brightens when you put something right.
- **It cheers in the big celebrations** for a project achieved and a reward bought.
- **A focus buddy** under the timer: determined while it runs, calm on pause, stretching on a break.
- **In the first setup** it waves, and if you choose **The game** you pick its skin, hair and eyes right there.
- **On the balance and streaks widget** (Android): turn on **Character on the balance and streaks widget** in **Settings → Appearance** to see its face there, happier as the day gets done.

**Reminders that follow your day**

- **A reminder can say what's at stake:** "Last one for today" or "This one makes today count for your streak: day 13".
- **The morning plan** starts with "Day 13 of your streak". **The evening check** starts with the closest win, like "1 more keeps your 12-day streak".
- **The Sunday summary** says when the week went better than the one before, and with **Beat last week** on, when you won.
- **A tick from a reminder or a widget answers back** (Android): a short note, "Done: Water, +5 · 2 left today", that goes away by itself. The switch is **Confirm a tick made outside the app** in **Settings → Notifications**, on to start.
- A private goal behind the lock is never named, and streaks are left out while the review is locked.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.71.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.71.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.71.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: if Checklist is running, quit it first. Then download `checklist-0.71.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
