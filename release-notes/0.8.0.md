Snooze and keep reminding, and backups that look after themselves.

**New**

- **Snooze on reminders.** Each reminder has a **Snooze** button. It comes back after 10 minutes, unless the goal is done by then. Choose 10 minutes, 30 minutes or an hour in **Settings → Notifications**.
- **Keep reminding until it's done.** In a habit's editor, under Reminders, turn on **Keep reminding until it's done**: the reminder comes again every 30 minutes until you tick it or its finish time passes.
- **+1 on reminders** for goals you do several times a day, so you can count one without opening the app.
- **Backup reminder.** When your last backup is over a week old, Today shows a card with **Back up** and **Later**. Turn it off in **Settings → Backup**.
- **Automatic backup.** In **Settings → Backup**, tap **Choose a folder**. Each day you open the app, it saves a dated backup file there and keeps the last 7. Settings shows when the last backup was made.

Choose a folder on the phone itself, like Documents. If you pick a folder that another app copies online, your backups go online with it.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.8.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
