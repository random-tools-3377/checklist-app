Bills, saving up, a report for each month, and a budget game.

**New in the Budget tab** (with Budget on, in Settings → Features)

- **Bills and subscriptions.** Under **Bills coming up**, tap **Add bills** (or **All bills**) and add rent, phone, streaming and the like: what it's for, how much, how often (every so many weeks or months), from which date, and when it ends if it does. Each one counts as spent on its day, by itself, in its category (Bills unless you pick another). The card shows what's due in the next 30 days, and the list shows what they all come to in a month. A new bill counts from the day you add it; if one was already paid this month, add it as spending.
- **Saving for something.** Under **Saving for**, tap **Add**, say what it's for and how much it costs. **Put in** and **Take out** move money in and out, and a bar fills up, with how much is left and how long that takes at your pay. Money you put in comes off what's left in the month.
- **A report for each month.** **Report for …** at the bottom of the Budget tab: pay, other money in, spending, savings and what's left; what share of your pay you spent; a chart of each day's spending; and where it all went, by category. The arrows go back to earlier months.
- **Today's pay on Today.** A small bar under your balance fills up as you work, with how long is left. Tap it to open the Budget tab. You can move or hide it in Settings → Appearance like the other parts of Today.

**New: Budget game** (Settings → Features → Budget game, with Budget on)

- **Each day gets a share.** Your month's pay after tax, plus other money in, less your bills, spread evenly over the month. The **Money game** card in the Budget tab shows today's share, what you've spent against it, and how much is left.
- **Points for good money days.** Spend no more than the day's share and you get **+3 points**; spend nothing at all and it's **+5**. Bills and money put into savings don't count against the day; rewards you buy in the shop do. Points come in the next time you open the app on a new day, and show in your points history.
- **A money streak.** Good money days in a row show as 🔥 on the card.
- **Bonuses.** A month where every category with a limit stayed within it pays **+25** when it ends. Reaching a saving goal pays **+20**, once, with a little celebration.
- It starts from the day you turn it on; earlier days don't count.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.58.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.58.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.58.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.
