Hard days feel like progress.

**Changed** (with **Character check-ins** and the **Mood check-in** on)

When you tell your character it's a hard day, it suggests one small thing. Before, once you'd done it, it gave you the same nudge for the next thing. Now:

- **Each thing you do counts as a win.** The character's card shows a 🌱 for each one ("2 small wins today"), and the character does a little hop.
- **Its words move on with you.** After the first, it says you did it and that it counts double on a hard day. You can stop there, or do one more small thing if you like. After two, it calls it real progress. From three, it's proud of you.
- **The small thing it suggests** is still the quickest goal left, and there's always the button to tick it from the card.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.54.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.54.0.apk` too.
