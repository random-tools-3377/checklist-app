Skips, misses and the limit, penalty tasks, streaks, and a privacy lock.

**New**

- **Skip.** Each goal on Today has a Skip link. A skip needs no reason. A skipped goal can still be ticked later.
- **The allowance.** Each goal has a number of skips and misses it allows, per week or per month (it starts at 1 a week). Set it in the goal's editor. It starts fresh each Monday, or on the 1st for a monthly allowance.
- **Misses.** When the app opens, it checks for goals that were neither done nor skipped in time. Each miss needs a reason: forgot, no time, ill, or chose not to, with an optional note. If you did it but forgot to tick it, tap "I did it, I forgot to tick it": the goal is ticked and any penalty is given back. A project that misses its deadline counts as a miss too.
- **Over the limit.** Going past a goal's allowance costs half the points the goal pays, and there's a penalty task to do right away. Either make up one of the goals you skipped or missed recently, or draw one of your own penalty tasks at random. Write your penalty tasks in **Settings → Penalty tasks**. Penalty tasks pay nothing. Before a skip goes over the limit, the app warns you and asks for a reason.
- **Streaks** on Today: days the main goal was reached, days with no misses, no skips, within the limit, and perfect days. Choose what a day needs in **Settings → Streaks**: a number of goals, or a share of what was due (it starts at 90%). When every streak is going at once, it shows big.
- **Skip on notifications.** Reminders now have a Skip button next to Done. A skip that would go over the limit opens the app instead, for the warning, the reason and the penalty task.
- **Privacy lock.** In **Settings → Privacy**, choose what sits behind the lock: the points history, the shop and bought rewards, or goals you mark private in their editor. Unlocking uses your phone's own fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern, and stays open until you leave the app. While locked, private goals are left off the widget and their notifications say "A private goal".
