A fix for the pace card, and the first money and shop changes.

**Fixed: "Not enough time" when there was plenty**

- A goal that was already late, like one set for 7:45 AM seen at 10 AM, made the pace card on Today say "Not enough time: 2 h 20 min short". Now a late goal just shows as late on its own card, and the warning only appears when what's left really doesn't fit before your finish time.

**Rewards and money** (with **Budget** on in Settings → Features)

- **Any reward can have a money value.** Buying it counts as spending under Rewards. Its points price only follows the money if you turn on **Price follows what it costs**.
- **The Buy box shows the money side:** what the reward costs, and how much of your monthly Rewards limit that leaves. It warns you if you'd go over.
- **Pay from savings.** If a saving goal holds enough, the Buy box lets you pay from it. The money comes out of that goal and doesn't count as this month's spending. Returning the reward puts it back.

**Savings**

- **Last month's leftover can go into savings.** At the start of a month, the Budget tab shows what you had left and offers **Move to savings**: into one of your saving goals, or into a plain **Savings** account if you have none, so you can watch it grow.

**Shop**

- **Undo after "Yes, used it"**, in case of a slip.
- **A Used list** under Bought shows the rewards you've enjoyed, latest first.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.67.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.67.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.67.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: download `checklist-0.67.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
