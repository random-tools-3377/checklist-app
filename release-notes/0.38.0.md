Weekly planning, and a year in review.

**New**

- **Weekly planning** (an extra: turn it on in **Settings → Features**). From Monday, a card on Today offers a few minutes of planning:
  - last week summed up, with the goal you missed most and your best one
  - this week's points target to set
  - the days left in the week, at a glance, with a button to move things in the planner
  - **Done planning** puts the card away until next Monday, and so does **Not this week**
- **Year in review.** The **Review** tab has a **Year** view. It shows each month's share of what got done, the points earned, and the month's average mood if you use the mood check-in.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.38.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
