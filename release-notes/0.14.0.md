Mystery rewards, and a widget for your balance and streaks.

**New**

- **Mystery rewards.** In the shop, add a reward and turn on **Mystery reward**, then write what it could be, like "Coffee out", "Film night" or "New book". Each time you buy it, one is drawn at random and revealed. It waits under **Bought** like any reward, and can be returned for its price until you use it.
- **Balance and streaks widget.** A second home-screen widget, "Checklist balance and streaks": your balance, your streak, and how much of today is done, plus the weekly target if you set one. Make it wider to see every streak. Tap it to open the app. To add it, hold an empty spot on the home screen, choose **Widgets**, and find Checklist.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.14.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
