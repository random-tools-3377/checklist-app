Choose what Today shows and in what order, and which tabs you see.

**New**, in **Settings → Appearance**

- **Today's sections.** Tick to show or untick to hide the balance, the weekly target, the streaks, the pace, and each list of goals (due today, this week, this month, this year, projects). Use the arrows to put them in your own order.
- **Tabs.** Hide the Goals, Shop or Review tab if you don't use it. A hidden tab can still be opened from the top of **Settings**.
- **Opens on.** Choose the tab the app opens on.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.17.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
