Clearer ticking, every emoji, and times for goals you do more than once a day.

**New**

- **Clear tick buttons.** Each goal on Today has a button that says what it does: **Done** for a single goal, **+1** for one you do several times, with "1 of 2" underneath. Once it's done the button fills in with a check mark; tap a single goal's button again to take the tick back.
- **Every emoji.** Tap a goal's or reward's icon to choose from the full standard set, grouped like a keyboard, with a search ("dog", "book") and a skin tone choice. Your recent picks come first. Emoji your phone can't show are left out.
- **"My day starts at"** in **Settings → Your day**.
- **Goals you do several times a day.** In the editor, turn on **A time for each one** to give each its own time, like 9 am and 9 pm; each gets its own reminder. Leave it off and the app spreads them out evenly between when your day starts and the finish time. Either way, Today shows when the next one is due, like "Next one by 2:30 pm".
- **Morning plan.** When your day starts, one notification sums up today: how many goals, what to do first, and the pace. Turn it off in **Settings → Notifications**.

Spreading out the times and the morning plan both need "My day starts at" set first.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.7.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
