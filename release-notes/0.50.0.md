Paths and titles for your character.

**New** (with **Character and skills** on)

- **Choose a path at level 10.** On the character page, pick one: 🏅 Athlete, 🌿 Healer, 🦉 Scholar, 🏡 Homemaker, 📈 Professional or 🛠️ Maker. Its skill gets 10% more experience, and its name becomes your title, shown beside your level on Today. You can change your path any time.
- **Titles to earn** from the way you play:
  - 🐦 Early bird: 20 ticks before 8 am
  - 🌙 Night owl: 20 ticks after 10 pm
  - ⚔️ Weekend warrior: everything done on 8 weekend days
  - 🚂 Unstoppable: a 50-day main streak
  - 🌈 All-rounder: every skill at level 5
  - 🗡️ Boss slayer: 4 bosses beaten
  - 🧭 Quest hunter: 30 quests finished
  - 🍀 Lucky star: 10 lucky ticks
- **Pick which title shows**: your path's, any you've earned, or none. The character page lists the ones still to earn and how.

Next: your character checks in when you fall behind, cheers you on, and responds to how you're feeling.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.50.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.50.0.apk` too.
