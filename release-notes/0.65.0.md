Your own sounds, an edge glow, a calendar widget that shows much more, and a tidier Today.

**Your own celebration sounds**

- **Pick your own sound** for a tick, for finishing a project and for buying a reward, in **Settings → Appearance → Celebrations**. Tap **Choose a sound…**, pick a sound file (up to 5 MB), and **▶ Play** to try it. **Use the app's sound** puts the usual one back.
- **A goal can have its own sound** too: **Sound when it's ticked** in its editor, under the celebration GIF.
- Your sounds go in your backup file, like your own GIFs.

**Edge lighting**

- **Edge glow:** when you finish something, a soft glow runs round the edges of the screen, in your colour or the goal's. It's quick for a tick, and longer and brighter for a project or a reward. It's on to start; the switch is under Celebrations.
- **Also light the edges when the app isn't on screen** (Android, off to start): a tick from a widget or a reminder shows a short, silent "Done" notification, which sets off your phone's own edge lighting if it has it.
- **Light up the screen for reminders** (Android, off to start, in **Settings → Notifications**): reminders then arrive the way a message does. Your phone's own **Edge lighting** setting decides how that looks.

**Calendar widget**

- **Wider, it shows the days ahead:** the next 7 days under headers like Today, Tomorrow and Fri 14, with project deadlines, to-dos and your phone's calendar events in one list. Each line shows its time and whether it's done, skipped or late.
- **Taller, it shows the month:** each day with how much was done, deadlines flagged, and today's list below.
- **+** adds a to-do or a goal straight from the widget.

**Reminders**

- **Project deadlines remind you** the day before and on the day, like "Due tomorrow, 3 steps left". Pick the time, or turn it off, in the project's editor.
- **The morning plan and weekly summary have their own notification types** on Android, so you can make them quiet in the phone's settings while goal reminders stay as they are.

**Today and planning**

- **Today, the Today widget, the quick settings tile and the morning plan now agree** on which goal comes first.
- **The day timeline is clearer:** each box is as tall as the goal takes, goals close together sit side by side, a line marks the time now, and each goal has its own Done button.
- **Plan your week** also lists goals you can do any day this week, this week's deadlines and to-dos, and your calendar's events.
- **To-dos can have a category**, and one typed on Today while a category is picked keeps it.
- **A to-do with a date** opens from Today or the planner, so you can change its date right there.
- **Goal cards have the same buttons everywhere:** weekly and monthly goals get Focus and Add a note too.

**Editors**

- **Leaving an editor with unsaved changes asks first:** "Discard your changes?"
- **Make a copy** saves your changes first.
- **Steps and checklist items** move with up and down arrows, and a ticked step keeps its tick.
- **Screens show your own numbers** from Points and rules everywhere, and a number out of range says what's allowed.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.65.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.65.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.65.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: download `checklist-0.65.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
