Call points what you like, pick a font, and set how celebrations feel.

**New**, in **Settings → Appearance → Personal**

- **Points are called…** Points, Coins 🪙, Stars ⭐, XP, Gems 💎, or your own words with an emoji, like "beans 🫘". The balance, the shop, the review, celebrations and the widgets all use the name.
- **Font**: the phone's own, Rounded, Easy to read (made for clear reading), or Serif.
- **Celebrations**
  - **Sound**: a short chime for a tick, a bigger one for finishing something or buying a reward. Off unless you turn it on. It plays at your phone's sound volume.
  - **Buzz**: on as before; turn it off if you'd rather not feel it.
  - **Confetti**: none, some (as before) or lots.
  - **A GIF for every goal**: pick one of the four that come with the app, and finishing any goal without its own GIF fills the screen with it.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.18.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
