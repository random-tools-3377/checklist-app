Rewards can be priced in money.

**New: prices from what a reward costs** (with **Suggested points and prices** on, in Settings → Features)

Open a reward in the shop and type what it costs in money, like 12 or 250. Its price in points works itself out, so a coffee and a weekend away each get a fair price, instead of one of four sizes.

- **How it's worked out.** Settings → Difficulty has your **weekly treat budget**: what you'd spend on treats in a week. Doing everything due in a week earns that much in rewards. So with a budget of $20, if a full week of your goals pays 400 points, $1 is 20 points and a $12 reward costs 240. If the budget isn't set yet, the reward editor asks for it.
- **It keeps in step.** Add goals, or make them pay more, and prices follow. They only move once what a full week pays has changed by more than a tenth, so they don't wobble from day to day. Changing the budget moves them straight away.
- **The shop shows both**, like "240 points · $12". Settings → Difficulty shows what $1 is worth in points now.
- **Your choice for each reward.** Typing a money value turns on "Price follows what it costs". Turn it off to set the price yourself; the money value then gives a suggestion, as before.
- **Rewards you already gave a money value** keep their price. Open one and turn on "Price follows what it costs" to switch it over.
- Things you've already bought keep what you paid.

The four sizes (Small treat, Medium, Big, Huge) are still there for rewards without a money value.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.56.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.56.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.56.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays. Setting up SideStore the first time is in the 0.55.0 release notes.
