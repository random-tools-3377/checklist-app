Reminders at your usual time, and habit chains. Both are extras: turn them on in **Settings → Features**.

**New**

- **Reminders at your usual time.** A goal with no set time and no reminder now reminds you around the time you usually tick it. The app learns that time from the last 4 weeks, once it has seen at least 5 ticks.
- **Habit chains.** In a habit's editor, **Comes after** picks the goal that comes before it, like Journal after Coffee. When you tick Coffee, a notification reminds you of Journal straight away, if it's due and not done yet. A chain can't loop back on itself.

**Good to know**

- Backup files made by this version can't be read by older versions of the app.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.36.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
