An optional AI that runs on your phone, with no internet, in a separate app.

**New**

- **Checklist AI**, a second app on this Releases page. It runs a small AI on your phone for the checklist app. It's completely optional: if you don't install it, nothing changes, and the checklist app stays the same size. Both apps are released together, with the same version number, every time.
- **Describe it.** With the AI on, a new habit starts with a box to describe it in your own words, like "read 20 pages every weekday at 9pm". Tap **Fill in** and the AI fills in the name, icon, days, time, length and amount below. Check it all, set the points, and save. Nothing is saved until you do.

**Setting up the AI (only if you want it)**

1. Download and install `checklist-ai-0.42.0.apk` from this page, as well as the usual `checklist-0.42.0.apk`.
2. Open **Checklist AI** and tap **Download in the browser**. Your browser downloads the AI file once: 614 MB, so Wi-Fi is best.
3. When it has finished, tap **Pick the file** in Checklist AI and pick it from your Downloads. It checks it's exactly the right file. Then you can delete the download.
4. In the checklist app, turn on **AI helper** in **Settings → Features**. It says when everything is ready.

Neither app can use the internet: the download happens in your browser, and everything you type stays on the phone. The AI is small, so it can get things wrong; that's why it only fills in the form for you to check.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.42.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. `checklist-ai-0.42.0.apk` is the optional AI app.
