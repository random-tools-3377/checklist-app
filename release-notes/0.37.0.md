A one-tap to-do widget, and changing several goals at once.

**New**

- **Quick to-do widget.** Add **Checklist quick to-do** to your home screen like any widget. One tap opens the app with a box to type a to-do in.
- **Select several goals.** On the **Goals** tab, tap **Select**, then tap the goals you want. Then give them all one category, set how hard they are, or remove them together. **Undo** brings removed goals back.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.37.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
