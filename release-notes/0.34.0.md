A streak bonus, and a daily mood check-in.

**New**

- **Streak bonus.** Keep your main streak going and goals pay more: 10% more from 7 days in a row, 20% from 30 and 30% from 100. It counts the streak up to yesterday, so it holds all day, and Today says when it's on. Turn it off in **Settings → Difficulty**.
- **Mood check-in.** Once a day, Today asks how you're feeling, with five faces; one tap saves it, or **Not today** puts it off until tomorrow. The **Review** shows each day's mood beside how much got done. Like reasons and notes, it's hidden while the privacy lock covers reasons. Turn it off in **Settings → Difficulty**.
- All the ways goals can pay more (difficulty, the boost and the streak bonus) multiply together, rounded down once.

**Good to know**

- Backup files now include moods. Older versions of the app can't read a backup made by this one.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.34.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
