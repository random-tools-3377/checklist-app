Help on every screen, every number yours to change, and a budget widget.

**New: help and tips**

- **ⓘ at the top of every screen** opens a short guide: what that part is for, and how to use it, with the real button names.
- **The first time you open each part**, its guide shows as a tip. Tap **Got it**, or **No more tips** to stop them.
- **Settings → Help and tips** has every guide in one place, a switch for the tips, and **Show every tip again**.

**New: Points and rules** (Settings → Points and rules)

Every number the app uses for points is now yours to change, each showing its usual value with a **Reset**, and **Reset all** at the top:

- **Penalties:** what going over the limit costs (half a goal's points, usually).
- **Difficulty:** what each level pays and how many extra free skips it gives.
- **Bonuses:** the boost, the streak bonus and the repeat-buy price rise.
- **The game:** quests, combos, bosses, lucky ticks, bets, shields and the path bonus.
- **The budget game:** what good money days and the bonuses pay.
- **Prices:** how far prices that follow money drift before they move, the balance check, and the suggested points and sizes.

Changes count from now on; nothing already paid changes.

**New: a budget widget** (Android)

Add **Checklist budget** from your home screen's widgets. It shows today's pay filling up and how long is left. Make it wider for this month's pay, what you've spent today, what's left and the next bill. Tap it to open the Budget tab. It updates when you add money and about every half hour, and shows the time it was last updated. If the budget is behind the privacy lock, the widget says so instead of showing it.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.59.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.59.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.59.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays. (The widget is Android only for now.)
