A calmer calendar widget: bigger when you add it, and you choose what it shows.

**Calendar widget**

- **Bigger by default.** Adding it now takes a 4 by 4 space, which shows the whole month. You can still drag it smaller or larger. A widget already on your home screen keeps its size until you resize it or add it again.
- **Choose what it shows.** In **Settings → Appearance**, under Widgets, **Calendar widget shows** has a switch for each kind:
  - **Goals done each day**, the small count under every day. This is now off to start, so the month isn't crowded.
  - **Calendar events**
  - **Project deadlines**
  - **To-dos with a date**
  - **Daily goals in the list**, for the goals you do every single day.
- **More room in each day.** With the count off, a day can show two things before "+N".
- **Easier to tell apart.** Events are in your colour, deadlines start with 🚩, and to-dos are in plain text.
- **Tapping a day** lists only what's on that day.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.67.2.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.67.2.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.67.2.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: download `checklist-0.67.2.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
