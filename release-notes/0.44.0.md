Voice: tick a goal or add a to-do by saying it.

**New** (with **AI helper** on, and Checklist AI installed)

- **🎙 on Today.** Tap it and say "done" and a goal that's due today, like "done reading" or "I did the gym", or "add" and a to-do, like "add buy milk" or "remind me to call the bank". The app shows what it heard and asks before ticking or adding anything.
- **Voice needs its own small file.** Open **Checklist AI**: under **Voice**, tap **Download the speech file** (41 MB), then **Pick the speech file** once it has downloaded. Checklist AI checks it's exactly the right file. The 🎙 button appears on Today once it's ready.
- The microphone is asked for the first time you use voice, by Checklist AI only. The checklist app itself never has the microphone. Your voice is turned into words on the phone as you speak and is never recorded or kept.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.44.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.44.0.apk` too: the two always go together.
