A check of the whole app, part by part, with 58 things put right. Nothing new to learn: things just work the way they say they do.

**Streaks and goals**

- **Copying a goal, giving it an earlier start, or bringing a removed one back** no longer breaks your streaks. A goal now counts only from the day it was added or brought back. A copy starts today.
- **Changing how often a goal repeats** partway through a week keeps the ticks you already made.
- **Switching a goal to "Some days after it's last done"** no longer makes it disappear.
- **A "3 times a week" goal started late in the week**, or ending early, no longer counts a short week as missed.
- **"I forgot to tick it"** now ticks everything that was left, like all 8 glasses or the rest of the week, and pays the bonus for finishing.
- The **reorder arrows** always move a goal now, and the planner's **Deadlines** show the steps done and leave out finished projects.

**Today**

- **"Perfect day so far"** only shows when the perfect-day bonus will really be paid.
- **The day timeline** puts a goal done several times a day at each of its times, and runs on past midnight when your day ends later.
- **When the focus timer runs out**, the goal is ticked once, with one "Time's up" note.
- **The pace card** counts what's really left: 6 glasses to go counts as 6.

**Misses and gaps**

- **"Times a week" goals** no longer bring up a false "you stopped for a while" check-in after a quiet weekend.
- **After a long time away**, one check-in covers it all, instead of a reason for every old miss.
- **Points given back** by "I forgot to tick it" no longer count toward a gap.

**Reminders**

- **A reminder keeps its time**, even if you change something in the minute before it.
- **Goals with an amount** keep reminding until the whole amount is entered. **A "times a week" goal** finished with a skip stops reminding.
- **Reminders go away** when their day ends or the goal is done, and their buttons no longer act on the wrong day.
- **Locked private goals** are never named in a reminder or the Sunday summary.
- **iPhone:** reminders now show while the app is open, the focus timer says "Time's up" even when the app is closed, and "keep reminding" no longer uses up the next days' reminders.

**Review**

- **Days still to come** no longer count as not done.
- **Removed goals** no longer count as due forever in the Sunday summary.
- **"Since last done" goals** now show in past weeks.
- **Points** in the review now add up, with interest and bets shown on their own.

**Privacy**

- **While private goals are locked**, they are left out of the review and last week's figures, and the points history and miss screens just say "A private goal".
- **While anything is locked**, changing a Privacy switch or exporting asks for your phone's lock first.
- **Every budget page** is now behind the budget lock, and locked pages close when you leave the app.

**The game**

- **Taking a tick back** now takes its lucky extra with it.
- **Bets** count from when they're placed and need more than you've already done.
- **The day's quests and the week's boss** no longer change partway through.
- **Ticks after midnight** no longer count as "before noon".
- **Titles you've earned** are kept.

**Mood**

- **"Mood and your goals"** now compares mornings with mornings, so a tired evening no longer puts every daily goal under "lower mood".
- **The tip on a low morning** no longer says a goal "lifts you".
- **Goals to quit** no longer get advice that doesn't fit them.
- **A mood note** you type is kept, even if you tap Done or Close without Save.

**Budget**

- **Monthly bills with an old first date** are counted again.
- **Turning the budget game off and on** no longer pays for the days it was off. Its daily share now takes off bills still to come, so it doesn't drop when a big bill arrives.
- **Night shifts:** after midnight, the pay card and widget show the shift that's still going.
- **Changed work days** in other months are no longer wiped when you open them.
- **Saving goals:** you can't take out more than is in one, and removing one asks first.
- **The pay editor** keeps your hours, and new bills still go under Bills after you rename it.

**Backups**

- **The automatic backup** no longer deletes exports you keep in the same folder. Its own files are now called `checklist-auto-…`.
- **A backup that fails** says so, instead of leaving an empty file.
- **iPhone:** cancelling "Save to" no longer says "Exported", and no stray copies are left behind.

**iPhone and Windows**

- **Settings that do nothing there**, like the morning plan, weekly summary and snooze, now say "Not on this device yet". Android-only options are left out.
- **Habit chains** show "Next up" on iPhone and Windows too.
- **On iPhone and Windows**, the icon picker only offers emoji the device can show.
- **On iPhone**, picking a photo that isn't a GIF says so.
- **Screen readers** now read every switch in Settings, and the whole row can be tapped.
- **The app opens on Today**, not Budget, while Budget is off.

**Installing**

Android: download `checklist-0.62.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.62.0.apk` too.

iPhone: download `checklist-0.62.0.ipa` below in Safari, then in SideStore go to My Apps, tap **+** and pick it. Your data stays.

Windows: download `checklist-0.62.0.msi` below and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
