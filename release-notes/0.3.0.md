Reminders at set times, and a calendar widget for your home screen.

**New**

- **Reminders, like a calendar's.** A habit with a set time notifies you at that time. In its editor you can turn that off, or add an earlier alert: 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes before. A habit without a set time can have a "Remind me at" time instead. Each notification has a **Done** button that ticks the goal without opening the app.
- **Calendar widget.** Long-press your home screen, choose Widgets, and add "Checklist calendar". Small, it shows today's goals with times and tick boxes. Make it wider to see this week, or taller to see the month: a full dot means everything due that day was done, a hollow dot means some was left.
- Reminders and the widget keep themselves up to date: after you tick something, change a goal, restart the phone or change the time zone.
- **Settings → Notifications** shows whether reminders are on, with a button to turn them on.

The first time you save a habit with a reminder, Android asks whether the app may send notifications. Everything is scheduled on the phone; nothing goes online.

**Not in this version yet**

- Skips, misses, the allowance, penalties and streaks, and the privacy lock. The Skip button on notifications comes with them.
- Gap check-ins, the review screen and stats.
- Celebration GIFs.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.3.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
