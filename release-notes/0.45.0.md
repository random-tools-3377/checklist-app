The start of a game: your character's level and six skills, and badge tiers.

**New**

- **Character and skills** (an extra: turn it on in **Settings → Features**, under **Game**). Every point a goal pays is experience for your character, so it levels up as you go. Your past ticks count from the start. Spending points and penalties never take experience away.
- **Six skills:** 🏃 Fitness, 💧 Health, 📚 Mind, 🏠 Home, 💼 Work and 🎨 Creative. Each goal belongs to one, guessed from its name and category, like "Morning run" for Fitness. Change it under **Skill** in the goal's editor. A goal's points level up its skill too, and a skill earns 🥉 bronze at level 5, 🥈 silver at 10 and 🥇 gold at 20.
- **Your level on Today**, under the balance, with how far to the next. Tap it for the character page with every skill.
- **Badge tiers.** Badges in a series now go 🥉 bronze, 🥈 silver, then 🥇 gold, with new ones at the top: 5,000 ticks and 12 weeks on target. With the character on, there are level badges too: levels 10, 25 and 50.

Next: your character drawn, with clothes and gear that change as each skill levels up.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.45.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data. If you use the AI, install `checklist-ai-0.45.0.apk` too.
