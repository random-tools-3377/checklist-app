Goals that repeat a few days after you last did them.

**New**

- **Some days after it's last done.** A new choice in a goal's **How often**: for things like watering the plants 5 days after the last time, whenever that was. Pick how many days, and when it's next due.
- When you tick it, the next one is due that many days later. Undo puts it back on today.
- If it isn't done on its day, that's one miss, with a reason as usual. Then it waits on Today, marked "waiting since", until you do it, with no more misses. The next one counts from the day you do it.

**Good to know**

- Backup files made by this version can't be read by older versions of the app.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.30.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
