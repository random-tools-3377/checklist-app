Items, a boss each week, and lucky ticks.

**New** (each an extra: turn it on in **Settings → Features**, under **Game**)

- **Items and bosses.** Every tick earns an item from its goal's skill: ⚡ energy bolts from Fitness, 🧪 potions from Health, 📜 scrolls from Mind, 🔨 hammers from Home, 📎 clips from Work and 🎨 paint bombs from Creative.
  - Each Monday a boss arrives on Today, like 🛋️ the Couch Lump or 🐉 the Deadline Dragon. Each one is weak to two skills, and items from those hit twice as hard.
  - Tap **Use items** to throw everything you have at it. Its health is set so an ordinary good week beats it, and beating it pays 25 points.
  - A boss still standing on Sunday night just leaves. You lose nothing, and you keep any items you haven't used.
- **Lucky ticks.** About one tick in ten is lucky: it pays its points again, or opens a chest of 5 to 15 points. The bar at the bottom says so. Undoing the tick takes the luck back too, so it can't be farmed.
