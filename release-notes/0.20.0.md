Goals with an amount, like 20 pages or 1 mile, entered as you go.

**New**

- **An amount in your own unit.** In a habit's editor, under **Amount**, set how much makes it done and what it's counted in: 20 pages, 3 chapters, 1 mile, 5 km, 30 reps, anything.
- **Enter how much you did.** On Today, tap **Add** and type what you did, like 12 pages now and 8 later. The card shows "12 of 20 pages" and fills up. The goal is done, and pays its points, when the amount is reached. The **−** button takes back your last entry.
- **How long it takes.** Either type a time to budget for the whole amount in the editor, or tap **▶ Start** on Today when you begin and **Stop** when you finish, then say how much you did. The app learns your usual speed from your own timings and plans your day with it: "12 of 20 pages · about 16 min left". A typed time wins until you clear it. The timing keeps running if you close the app.
- **Done** on a reminder or a tick in the calendar widget counts as doing what's left.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.20.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
