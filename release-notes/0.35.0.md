A tidier app: extras are now switched on only if you want them, and Settings can be searched.

**New**

- **Features page.** **Settings → Features** lists every extra with a switch, in three groups:
  - **Today:** to-dos, the timeline, the mood check-in and your phone's calendar
  - **Goals and rewards:** suggested points and prices, goals to quit, challenges and the balance check
  - **Points:** savings interest, the boost, the perfect-day bonus, the streak bonus and rising prices
- **Extras start off.** Today, the editors and the shop show only the core until you turn something on. Anything you already use stays where it is, like a to-do you have or a goal that's a challenge.
- **Empty things stay out of the way.** For example, there's no streak card until there's something to count.
- **Search in Settings.** Type at the top of Settings, like "backup" or "lock", to see just those settings.

**Good to know**

- The bonuses that were on by default in 0.27 to 0.34 (interest, the boost, the perfect-day bonus, the streak bonus and rising prices) are now off until you turn them on in **Settings → Features**. So is the mood check-in.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.35.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
