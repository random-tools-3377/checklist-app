A colour-blind friendly look.

**New**

- **Colour-blind friendly** (in **Settings → Appearance**, under the colours). Turn it on and:
  - good things are blue and bad things are orange: points earned and lost, a negative balance, running late, going over the limit. That pair stays easy to tell apart for every common kind of colour blindness, whatever colour you've picked for the app.
  - the month calendar in **Review** marks each day as well as colouring it: ✓ everything done, ◐ some, ✕ none, – a gap.
  - goals are offered colours that stay apart for colour-blind eyes.
  - the widgets follow along.
- Searching **Settings** for "colour blind" finds it.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.41.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
