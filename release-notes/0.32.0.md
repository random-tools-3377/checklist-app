Your phone's calendar in the planner and on Today.

**New**

- **Show my calendar.** Turn it on in **Settings → Phone calendar**. The phone asks once whether the app may read your calendar. If you have several calendars, you can pick which ones to show.
- Events then show in the **Plan** tab for each day, on the day **Timeline** as grey blocks, and on **Today** under **In your calendar**.
- **Pacing leaves your events out.** How long you have for each goal no longer counts time that's in your calendar, and Today says how much it left out.
- The calendar is only read, on the phone. Nothing is written to it or sent anywhere, and turning it off stops the reading.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.32.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
