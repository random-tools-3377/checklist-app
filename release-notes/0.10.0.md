A focus timer, a weekly summary, and quicker ways to set up goals.

**New**

- **Focus timer.** A goal with a length ("How long it takes" in its editor) shows **▶ Focus** on Today. It fills the screen with a countdown; when time's up the goal is ticked for you. You can pause, finish early with **Done now**, or stop without ticking. If you leave the app, a notification tells you when time's up.
- **Weekly summary.** On Sunday at 6 pm, a notification sums up your week: how much got done, the points, what was missed, and your best goal. Tap it to open the review. Turn it off in **Settings → Notifications**. With the review behind the privacy lock, it only says the review is ready.
- **Every few hours.** For a goal done several times a day with **A time for each one** on, tap **Every hour**, **2 h**, **3 h** or **4 h** to fill in the times in one go.
- **Make a copy of a goal**, from the bottom of its editor.
- **Bring back a removed goal.** Removed goals are listed at the bottom of the Goals tab, each with **Bring back**.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.10.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
