Your own reasons, notes on ticks, and fixing when something was done.

**New**

- **Your own reasons.** Besides forgot, no time, ill and chose not to, add your own reasons in **Settings → Reasons for skips and misses**, like "Travelling" or "Raining". They show up wherever a reason is asked for: misses, skips over the limit, and gap check-ins. The review counts them by their own words.
- **Notes on ticks.** Once you've ticked a goal, its card on Today shows **Add a note**. Write something like "ran 5 km". Notes show in the points history too.
- **Fix when it was done.** Tap a tick in the points history to change the time it was done (same day) or its note. The points stay the same.

**Installing**

Download `checklist-0.11.0.apk` below on the phone and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your data.
